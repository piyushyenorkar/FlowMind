## 1. LinkedIn Post

### Post Copy (Word Count: ~183 words)

Most Indian software teams don't stand up in pure English. We speak in natural, code-mixed Hinglish—and that is usually where critical decisions get lost.

To solve this, Debashree Mal and I built Voice Meetings for FlowMind, an AI project manager we previously co-created. For the @Gnani AI challenge, we integrated Gnani's sovereign speech intelligence directly into our meeting workflow.

Here is how it works:
During a standup, live audio streams to Gnani Prisma v3.0 (indic-auto). Prisma transcribes conversational Hinglish with telephony-grade accuracy—handling technical English terms and Hindi phrasing seamlessly, without saving any audio on our server.

FlowMind then feeds the transcript to Groq LLM to extract actionable deliverables, automatically matches them to team members based on their skill profiles, and commits them to Hindsight memory. To close the loop, Gnani Timbre v3.0 synthesizes crisp voice briefings in Hinglish.

Full disclosure: FlowMind's core task engine existed before, but the end-to-end Gnani-powered Voice Meetings feature was built from scratch for this hackathon.
