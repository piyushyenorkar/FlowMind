Production Recording Checklist

Complete these 6 checks before you hit record to ensure demo-day perfection.

✓

1080p / 1440p Clean Canvas

Set display resolution to 1920x1080 at 100% scale. Fullscreen browser (F11) to eliminate taskbars.

✓

Microphone Distance

Keep USB microphone 4–6 inches from your mouth, slightly off-axis to avoid harsh plosives.

✓

Notification Silence

Mute WhatsApp, Slack, and Discord desktop apps. Close unrelated browser tabs and bookmarks bar.

✓

Natural Dialogue Delivery

Speak the Hinglish lines at a natural conversational pace. Do not rush or over-enunciate technical terms.

✓

Zero Audio Storage

FlowMind streams memory buffers directly to Gnani APIs—zero raw audio files are stored or logged on disk.

✓

Background Music Levels

If adding music in post-production, keep background track volume under -20dB so dialogue stays crystal clear.
