## 📋 Recording Checklist & Guidelines

### Pre-Recording Setup:
1. **Screen Resolution**: Set display to **1920x1080 (1080p)** or **2560x1440 (1440p)** at 100% or 125% DPI scale. Zoom browser to 100% so text and badges are crisp.
2. **Audio & Mic**:
   - Use a directional USB mic or quality headset. Avoid laptop internal mic room echo.
   - Keep microphone 4-6 inches from your mouth, slightly off-axis to avoid plosives (*p/b* pops).
   - Record in a quiet room with minimal reverb (curtains, carpets help).
3. **Browser Cleanliness**:
   - Fullscreen browser window (`F11` or clean browser frame).
   - Close unrelated tabs, bookmarks bar, and system taskbar popups.
   - Turn off Slack, Discord, and WhatsApp desktop notifications.
4. **What to AVOID**:
   - Do NOT rush the Hinglish dialogue; speak naturally and clearly.
   - Do NOT show API keys, `.env` files, or personal numbers/emails on screen.
   - Do NOT use background music that overpowers voice dialogue. Keep music volume below -20dB if used.
