Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FlowMind ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://flowwithmind.vercel.app. FlowMind is an AI-powered project management platform built to help teams collaborate, track deliverables, log decisions, and streamline engineering workflows.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, password (securely hashed), and profile avatar.
  • Google Account Data (OAuth): When you sign in using Google, we access your Google primary email, your display name, and your profile picture to authenticate you and build your member profile.
  • Workspace & Project Data: Task titles, descriptions, assignees, deadlines, team decisions, meeting transcripts, and chat interactions.
  • Repository Data: Public repository metadata (commits, PRs, stars) when linking your GitHub repository.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve the FlowMind workspace.
  • To authenticate sessions and manage team permissions.
  • To power AI assistant capabilities and meeting summaries.
  • To maintain platform security and prevent unauthorized access.

4. Google API User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy: FlowMind's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We only request basic read permissions (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile).
  • We do not sell or transfer your Google user data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • We do not use Google user data to serve targeted advertisements.
  • We do not use your private personal data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely via Supabase PostgreSQL with encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, protected by Row Level Security policies.
